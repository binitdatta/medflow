Healthcare Agentic AI Platform

Intelligent healthcare workflows. Governed by identity.

MedFlow demonstrates how Agentic AI can assist healthcare professionals while operating inside enterprise security, authorization, human oversight, and audit boundaries.

Role-Aware AI Agents

Nursing, physicians, pharmacy, admissions, finance, management, and compliance receive capabilities appropriate to their authenticated role.

Human-in-the-Loop

Sensitive actions can require explicit human review and confirmation before the AI workflow is allowed to continue.

HIPAA-Focused Controls

Identity, least privilege, departmental separation, PHI-aware access patterns, and security boundaries are part of the architecture.

Detailed Auditability

Application activity, API operations, AI usage, and security-relevant events can be recorded for investigation and governance.

MedFlow is an educational Proof of Concept. It demonstrates HIPAA-focused architecture and responsible Enterprise AI design patterns rather than representing a certified production healthcare system.
