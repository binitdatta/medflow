About This Project

What MedFlow Is

MedFlow is a proof-of-concept agentic AI system built to demonstrate — not just describe — how a hospital group can give clinical and business staff a single conversational assistant, without collapsing the role boundaries that healthcare data requires.

Rather than one generic chatbot, MedFlow routes every conversation through a role-specific LangGraph subgraph, determined entirely by the staff member's Keycloak login — never by anything typed in chat. A Nurse's assistant can check bed status and record medication administration; a Physician's can review charts, order labs, and draft discharge summaries; a Legal reviewer's can only read the audit trail. Seven clinical/business roles are live, plus a dedicated HIPAA compliance reviewer role that can inspect every actual HTTP call made to the underlying LLM.

Every PHI-touching action is audited at the moment it happens, every LLM call is logged with cost and token detail, and de-identification is enforced at the API layer — not just assumed — for roles like Finance and Management that have no business seeing clinical detail.

Stack
  • Backend: Python 3.12, Flask, SQLAlchemy
  • Database: MySQL 8.x
  • Agent orchestration: LangChain + LangGraph
  • LLM: Anthropic Claude
  • Identity: Keycloak 26 (PKCE + confidential client)
  • UI: Bootstrap 5, Chart.js

Roles & Skills

Nurse

Unit patient lists, medication schedules, bed status, recording administrations.

Pharmacist

Active medication review, drug interaction checks, inventory, dispensing.

Admissions

Patient lookup/registration, encounters, bed assignment.

Physician

Full chart review, diagnoses, lab orders, prescriptions, discharge summaries.

Finance

Billing claims and revenue summaries — no clinical detail ever exposed.

Management

Rolled-up occupancy, encounter volume, inventory, and revenue across hospitals.

Legal

Read-only audit trail review — who accessed what, and when.

HIPAA Admin

Compliance reviewer — inspects every real LLM call for PHI handling.
