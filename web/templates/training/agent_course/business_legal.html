13. Management, Finance and Legal

Understand aggregate-only, billing-only and audit-only boundaries.

Course map

Management

Occupancy, encounter volume, inventory and revenue summaries.

Aggregate only

Finance

Claims, payer, status and revenue totals.

No clinical detail

Legal

Patient, staff and general audit-trail queries.

Read-only evidence

When reporting results, state exactly what the audit trail shows — who,
what action, what resource, and when — without speculating about intent or
drawing conclusions about whether an access was appropriate. That judgment
belongs to the human reviewing the report.
Data minimization

These are not merely different chat personalities. Their API payloads and toolsets intentionally expose different categories and levels of detail.
